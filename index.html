What happens in the studio

A calm introduction to modelling the human form

Lucie introduces clay modelling techniques and guides guests through making their first sculpture. The class can begin from Lucie's own figures, or from images guests bring for a personal idea.

  • 3 hours of teaching in Lucie's Copenhagen atelier
  • 2.5 kg clay included
  • Firing of the finished sculpture included
  • Coffee, tea, and a light serving
  • Glaze firing available as an add-on

French artist in Copenhagen

Sculpture, portraits, color, and hands in clay

Lucie Verdet is inspired by faces, bodies, dance, flowers, vibrant colors, and homes. Her workshops are intimate because the work is tactile and personal: each guest has time to ask, shape, adjust, and discover.
